The kitchen

A small room with a big fire

Everything on the menu meets the hearth at some point: vegetables blistered in the embers, whole fish cooked on the grill, bread baked in the oven while it cools down overnight. The menu is short and changes every Tuesday, so there is always something new to try.

We work with six farms within an hour of the restaurant and buy whole animals, which means the snacks and specials make use of every cut. Ask your server what came in this morning.
