Since 2019

One hearth, six farms, a lot of friends.

{{site_title}} began as a Sunday supper club in a borrowed backyard. Five years later the fire is indoors, but the idea is the same: cook simply, cook seasonally and feed people well.

What we believe in

Fire first

There is no gas in our kitchen. Oak and fruitwood give every plate a little smoke and a lot of character.

Close to the farm

Our growers are all within an hour's drive. We plan the menu around their harvest, not the other way round.

A fair kitchen

Four-day weeks for the whole team, shared tips and a family meal at four every afternoon.

The people behind the fire

Head chef Mara Okafor cooked in coastal kitchens for a decade before coming home to Port Alder. Her partner Theo Lindqvist runs the floor and the wine list, which leans towards small growers who farm without shortcuts. Together with a kitchen of seven and a floor team of six, they open the doors five nights a week.

If you would like to cook with us, we take one stage each season. Write to us with a little about yourself.
