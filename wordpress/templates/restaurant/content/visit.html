14 Harbour Lane, Port Alder

Visit and reservations

Book up to thirty days ahead for groups of up to six. The bar and the counter by the fire are kept for walk-ins every night.

Book a table

Call us on (555) 014-2290 between 2 pm and 6 pm, or email tables@emberandrye.example with your name, date, time and number of guests. We confirm every booking within a day.

Running late? Give us a ring and we will hold your table for fifteen minutes.

Getting here

We are two minutes from the Harbour Lane tram stop. Street parking is free after 6 pm, and there is a bike rack right outside the door.

Opening hours

  • Monday Closed
  • Tuesday – Thursday 5 pm – 10 pm
  • Friday – Saturday 12 pm – 11 pm
  • Sunday 12 pm – 9 pm

The kitchen takes last orders thirty minutes before closing.

Private dining

The long table, for up to eighteen

Birthdays, team dinners and small weddings at one table beside the fire. Set menus start at $65 per guest, with wine pairings from $40.

Email events@emberandrye.example and we will send menus and available dates.

Good to know

Do you cater for allergies?

Yes. Tell us when you book and the kitchen will plan ahead. Most plates can be made without gluten or dairy.

Are children welcome?

Always, especially for weekend lunch. We have high chairs and a small plate of whatever the grown-ups are having.

Is the restaurant accessible?

The dining room and restroom are step-free. Let us know if you need a table with more space and we will set it up.
