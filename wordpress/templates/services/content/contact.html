FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Is the quote really free?

Yes. Phone and email quotes are always free, and so are site visits for jobs we expect to cost more than $300. You only pay once you have approved a price and the work is done.

How soon can you come out?

Most small jobs are booked within 48 hours. Renovations usually start two to four weeks after you accept the quote. Emergencies are answered around the clock.

Do I need to be home during the job?

Someone needs to let us in and agree the price. After that, many customers leave a key with us or head to work, and we send photos of the finished job.

Can I supply my own parts or paint?

Of course. We will check they are suitable before fitting them. Our workmanship guarantee still applies, while the parts are covered by their maker’s warranty.

How do I pay?

By card on the day, or by bank transfer within seven days of the emailed invoice. Renovations are billed in stages, with no more than 20% due before work begins.

What if something goes wrong after you leave?

Call us. Every job carries our 2-year workmanship guarantee, so if a fault comes from our work we return and fix it free of charge.
