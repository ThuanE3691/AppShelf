Our story

It started with one van and a waiting list

In 2013 Dana Okafor, a plumber with fifteen years on commercial sites, kept hearing the same complaint from friends: nobody would come out for a leaking tap or a door that would not close. So she bought a second-hand van, printed a price list and started taking the jobs everyone else ignored.

Word spread through Port Alder faster than she could answer the phone. Today {{site_title}} runs four vans and covers every trade a home needs, but the rules are the same as on day one: show up when we say we will, price the job before we start and leave the place cleaner than we found it.

Guarantees

Promises we put in writing

2-year workmanship guarantee

If something we fixed fails because of our work within two years, we come back and put it right at no cost. Parts carry the maker’s warranty on top.

Upfront, fixed pricing

You agree the price before work starts. If we find something unexpected, we stop, show you and ask before doing more.

On time, or $50 off

We book two-hour arrival windows and text when we are on the way. If we are late without warning, $50 comes off your bill.
