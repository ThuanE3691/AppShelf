How our pricing works

  • Fixed price before we start. You approve the total, and that is what you pay, even if the job runs long.
  • Parts at supplier cost. Receipts are attached to your invoice. We never add a markup on materials.
  • Free travel in Port Alder. Outside our service area we add a flat $25 travel fee, agreed in advance.
  • Bundle and save. Several small jobs on one visit share a single call-out, so extra work is billed by time only.

Emergency call-outs outside opening hours start at $180, including the first hour. All prices include sales tax.
