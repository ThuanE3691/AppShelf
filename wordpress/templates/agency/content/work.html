01 · Bakery chain

Wheatwell Bakehouse

Wheatwell grew from one corner shop to fourteen, and every new branch arrived with a slightly different sign, bag and menu board. We rebuilt the identity around the wheat stamp from their very first paper bags, drew a sturdy logotype for shopfronts and designed packaging any store manager can print locally. The new website puts today’s bake and click-and-collect first.

  • Brand identity
  • Packaging
  • Shopfronts
  • Website
  • +31% click-and-collect orders in the first six months
  • 1 system replacing 23 one-off bag and box designs

02 · Climate tech

Tidelight Carbon

Tidelight measures how much carbon coastal kelp farms remove from the air, and has to explain it to investors, regulators and fishing communities at once. We wrote the site in plain language before drawing a single screen, then built a calm, evidence-first identity and a public dashboard where every number links to its method.

  • Brand strategy
  • Product design
  • Data visualisation
  • Content design
  • 2.4× more time spent on the methodology pages
  • 4 months from launch to a closed funding round

03 · Public library network

Alder Valley Libraries

Nine branches, three catalogue systems and a website that sent readers to the wrong one. We mapped every task people come to do, from renewing a loan to booking a meeting room, and rebuilt the site around those tasks. A friendly identity and new wayfinding signage followed, tested with readers aged 8 to 88.

  • UX research
  • Website
  • Wayfinding
  • Accessibility
  • 46% fewer help-desk calls about renewals and holds
  • WCAG 2.2 AA across the site, checked with screen-reader users

04 · Cycling brand

Pennant Cycles

A small-batch steel frame builder with a loyal following and an online shop that only really worked on a desktop. We built a bolder identity on the shape of a race pennant, art-directed the bikes against flat colour and rebuilt the shop mobile-first, with a frame-size finder that asks three questions instead of twelve.

  • Brand identity
  • E-commerce
  • Art direction
  • UX
  • +58% mobile conversion rate in the first quarter
  • 40% fewer returns for the wrong frame size
