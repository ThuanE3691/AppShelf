Starting a project

What to put in your first email

  • What you are making, and who it is for
  • Any date we should plan around, such as a launch, an opening or a funding round
  • A rough budget, even if it is only a range
  • Links to work you admire, and anything you want to leave behind

No brief yet? That is fine. Half of our projects begin with a 30-minute call where we write the brief together.

Process

What happens after you write

  1. Intro call A free 30-minute call to understand the problem, the people and the timeline.
  2. Proposal Within a week, a written plan with scope, schedule and a fixed price. No surprises later.
  3. Discovery Workshops, interviews and an audit of what you have, ending in the one idea the work must carry.
  4. Design and build Two-week rounds with a review at the end of each, so you see real progress rather than a big reveal.
  5. Launch and care We launch together, train your team and check in again at 30 and 90 days.
