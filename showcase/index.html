Portfolio project · SKU WP-01

Hosted WordPress sites, off the shelf.

AppShelf is a personal demo project: a small SaaS that sells hosted WordPress sites. Pick a template, pay with a Stripe test card, and a background worker builds your site on a WordPress Multisite network in about a minute. Stripe runs in test mode only, so no real card is charged. The live stack runs on demand; this page keeps static snapshots of its three templates.

How a purchase becomes a site.

Every step is a real Stripe event or background job in the live stack. Nothing on this page talks to it: these snapshots are plain files.

  1. 01

    Stripe Checkout

    checkout.session.completed

    The customer picks a template and site name, then pays on a Stripe-hosted Checkout page with a test card.

  2. 02

    Webhook

    POST /api/stripe/webhook

    The Next.js app verifies the event, records the subscription once per event id, turns the licence on and creates a pending site.

  3. 03

    Jobs

    site.provision · SKIP LOCKED

    A job row in Postgres hands the work to the worker, with retries and backoff. Cancel, renew and the 48-hour cleanup are jobs too.

  4. 04

    wp-cli

    site-create.sh

    The worker runs the site scripts: a new blog on the network, the chosen block theme, its pages and menu, and a one-time admin password.

  5. 05

    WordPress Multisite

    wp.…/your-site/

    The site is live on the shared network, and the dashboard shows its address and admin login.
